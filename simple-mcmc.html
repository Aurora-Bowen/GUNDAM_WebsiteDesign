Simple MCMC

Simple MCMC explores the likelihood or posterior distribution by generating a Markov chain of parameter points.

Unlike direct minimization, MCMC characterizes the multidimensional parameter distribution rather than returning only a single best-fit point.

Chain Configuration

The MCMC configuration determines how the chain is initialized, how long it runs, and how samples are stored.

Concept Purpose
Burn-in Initial chain period discarded before collecting the final posterior sample.
Cycles Controls the number and length of MCMC sampling periods.
Starting point Defines the initial parameter state used to begin the chain.
Output Controls storage of chain states, likelihood values, and diagnostics.

Proposal and Adaptation

The proposal mechanism determines how candidate parameter points are generated from the current chain state.

Adaptive proposal methods can update their covariance or step behavior using information gathered during the chain.

Burn-in

Burn-in allows the chain to move away from its initial state before posterior samples are used for inference.
